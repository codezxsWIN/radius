# Neutrality and Permanent Scope Boundaries

Blast Radius is not a product and will not become one. Its only project outputs are an openly specified cross-platform metric, public conformance suite/reference implementations and a planned open aggregate dataset. Anyone can implement or test the standard without permission, vendor affiliation, a fee or a trademark licence. Passing tests supports a versioned self-claim, not certification.

## Independence

The proposed steering group has five seats and no more than one seat per vendor/affiliate, including the founder's Microsoft affiliation. Founder and member recusals, independent second review for vendor-employed connector maintainers, and a25% sponsor-share cap are mandatory in GOVERNANCE.md. Sponsors have no authority over definitions, cohort selection or findings. A sponsor cannot purchase early raw-data access or suppress an unfavorable result.

The project currently has NO accepted neutral host, elected independent steering group, independently approved privacy protocol or real tenant dataset. These absences are release blockers, not details to hide. The first real public aggregate must wait for signed neutral custody and the privacy/ethics/deduplication gates. OpenSSF is a candidate route with a documented specification/data licensing issue, not an established affiliation.

## Permanent Non-Goals

SIEM and ticketing connectors; tool SSO; multi-tenant consoles; compliance packs; sales/lead capture; paid tiers; certification fees; executive dashboards beyond the single preserved demo. No service control plane, automatic policy remediation, monetized dataset or proprietary scoring extension will be developed under this project.

The existing local what-if calculation is a research counterfactual and a single-demo aid, not a remediation product roadmap. Existing SARIF is a legacy local serialization format, not a commitment to an integration ecosystem. Offline provider adapters are auditable normalization experiments; completeness is not implied by the platform's name.

## Fair Comparisons

Credit attack-graph, authorization-reasoning, CIEM and open-source predecessors. Do not call this the first or only metric/dataset without a defensible novelty census. Vendor-owned and community-owned implementations receive identical tests. A platform is never assigned a worse score merely because of its brand, and rankings with incompatible inventories/models are forbidden.

Publish negative controls, undefined values, failed conformance cases, unsupported semantics and missing evidence. Never call synthetic distributions anonymized real-world observations, or present a reconstructed one-credential graph as a historical top-decile finding. Contributors may challenge any source mapping without being customers or sponsors.

## Free Operation Boundary

Artifacts and self-testing are free forever. This does not guarantee that a hosting provider will supply unlimited storage, compute or bandwidth without cost. The default distribution is static downloadable artifacts and offline execution; any future aggregate hosting must have an explicit public budget and remain within diversified, non-influencing support. No dependency on a paid certification or proprietary API is permitted for the core claim.
