# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors and critics regardless of identity, background, employer, experience or ability to pay. Participation requires respectful, evidence-focused collaboration in repositories, meetings, reviews, events and project-representative communications.

## Expected Conduct

Discuss claims and reproducible evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, credit others' work, disclose conflicts, respect privacy, and accept corrections. Reviewers may challenge the metric, privacy analysis, sponsor relationship or founder's decisions without retaliation. Criticism does not require proposing a replacement or agreeing with the maintainer.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discriminatory exclusion, threats, sexualized attention, disclosure of private information, impersonation, retaliation, intimidation, knowingly misleading research claims and repeated disruptive personal attacks are prohibited. No one may publish credentials, personal data or confidential tenant details to prove a point. Good-faith technical disagreement is not misconduct.

## Reporting and Independence

Before inviting external participants, the neutral host must publish a private reporting channel and at least two trained independent conduct contacts. No email address, appointment or host is fabricated in this draft; that reporting route is currently **not established**, and external community launch is blocked until it exists. Do not put sensitive conduct reports into public issues.

A report involving the founder, employer or a conduct contact must be handled by an unconflicted contact and the host, not the subject of the report. Confidentiality is limited to what investigation and safety require, with consent and minimum disclosure. Reports, evidence and retention periods are handled separately from public technical decision records.

## Response and Appeal

The conduct team acknowledges receipt, offers a safe point of contact, assesses immediate risk and records a proportionate response. Available actions include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from a role or a participation ban. Explain the basis and duration to affected parties where safe; do not promise an outcome or anonymity that cannot be delivered.

Appeals go to an unconflicted host representative who did not decide the original case. Publish only aggregate enforcement statistics and policy changes, not identifying case details. Seat removal and technical voting consequences must also follow GOVERNANCE.md. Neither sponsorship nor founding status grants immunity.
