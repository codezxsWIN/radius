# Preregistered NHI Synthetic Sanity Check

These are illustrative results from500 paired fictional configurations per regime, not evidence about real enterprise tenants. PREREGISTRATION.md and RUN_PLAN.md were hashed before generation; the receipt records internal ordering but is not an external registration. No source-backed cloud parameter distribution was available, so none is invented.

## Executed Primary Results

| Regime | Joint successes | Fraction | 95% Wilson interval | Unchanged criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type-neutral null | 54/500 | 10.8% | [8.3723%,13.8255%] | Falsified in this synthetic population |
| NHI degree multiplied by8 | 250/500 | 50.0% | [45.6341%,54.3659%] | Inconclusive |
| Human degree multiplied by8 | 54/500 | 10.8% | [8.3723%,13.8255%] | Falsified in this synthetic population |

The joint event is NHI credential-bearing principal share<0.5 AND NHI sensitivity-weighted union reach>0.5. Every regime had250 NHI-minority tenants; no exclusions were needed in this fully controlled population. Thus even the NHI-broader control cannot exceed50% joint success in this sampled mixture. The criterion is intentionally not relaxed after seeing this result.

## Secondary Results and Criticism

| Regime | Mean NHI union | Mean human union | Mean overlap | Mean total union |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Null | 0.475261 | 0.566776 | 0.284900 | 0.757137 |
| NHI broader | 0.970525 | 0.566776 | 0.553108 | 0.984193 |
| Human broader | 0.475261 | 0.986035 | 0.470989 | 0.990307 |

In53 of the54 successful null tenants, humans also reached a majority of the weighted surface; in the reversed control all54 did. The joint hypothesis does not compare humans' and NHIs' relative authority, and therefore cannot alone establish disproportionate NHI privilege. Its result is unchanged when only humans become more powerful. This is an identified limitation of the research claim, not a reason to hide the negative control.

The mean within-tenant NHI/human median weighted radii were0.026202/0.025443 under the null,0.179282/0.025443 under NHI broadening, and0.026202/0.174983 under human broadening. These secondary quantities show the deliberately constructed type advantage much more directly, but are not substituted for the registered primary outcome.

## Interpretation and Next Protocol

Retain the original outcome and its interval. A future independently registered study could ask whether NHI per-principal reach exceeds human per-principal reach within the same tenant, report a paired effect size, and stratify the minority condition separately. It would need power analysis, eligibility rules and sensitivity profiles fixed before data collection. This is a prospective proposal, not a post-hoc positive result.

The planned structural payload cannot recover unique-pair union overlap. Real-data testing of this primary question is blocked until a separately versioned, bounded, locally computed statistic passes privacy review. No real-data hypothesis is accepted or rejected here; no prevalence estimate, p-value for a real population, or claimed agent-specific industry result is reported.

Reproduce with `python tools/run_research.py`; inspect research/illustrative-nhi-results.json for every tenant-level value and research/tenant-configurations.jsonl for all1500 graph hashes. Matched controls share the same500 identity/resource populations. The seed and designed ranges are fully disclosed; they are not an anonymized sample of actual organizations.
