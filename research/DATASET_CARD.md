# Dataset Card: Synthetic Structural-Privacy Experiment

Version1.0-draft; licence CC0-1.0 under ../LICENSING.md. Creator: Blast Radius contributors. DOI: NOT ASSIGNED. Suggested citation includes this title, version, protocol hash and repository/archive location; no public URL or DOI is fabricated.

## Contents and Origin

-500 synthetic tenant summaries in synthetic-summaries.jsonl, generated from a published seed and investigator-designed ranges.
-1500 paired configuration records for the null, NHI-broader and human-broader regimes; these are500 underlying populations with three grant interventions, not1500 independent tenants.
-Per-tenant synthetic NHI outcomes, uniqueness/nearest-neighbor/utility results, a seeded illustrative noisy aggregate and a pre-generation protocol receipt.
-One full fictional example graph. Other graphs are recreated deterministically from tenant index, regime and seed; graph hashes are included.

No actual organization, real identity, secret value, discovered tenant policy or submitted enterprise summary appears here. No human participant was enrolled. Source incident narratives are a separate literature-based arm, not the source of these generated distributions.

## Intended Use and Limits

Use for reproducibility, schema testing, failure demonstrations and release-policy development. Do not use as estimates of cloud identity prevalence, empirical permission distributions, historical incident radius, tool accuracy on real tenants or evidence supporting the real NHI hypothesis.

The generator omits nested groups, denies, conditional policies and escalation; those appear in the separate conformance suite. The37-dimensional exact summary was unique for every generated tenant. Low-dimensional noisy aggregates sacrifice the rare tail. These observations characterize this design only.
