# Artifact Licensing

The project is free to use, implement, test and redistribute under these artifact-specific terms. These notices apply to original project material only; external source documents retain their authors' terms and are cited, not relicensed.

| Material | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Original reference implementations, runners, generators, tests, adapters and demonstration code | Apache License2.0; full text in LICENSE |
| Metric specification, normative registries, schemas, conformance fixture definitions and human derivations | Creative Commons Attribution4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0) |
| Original documentation, paper draft and reconstruction narratives | CC-BY-4.0 |
| Generated synthetic research rows, their statistical aggregates and future approved aggregate datasets | CC0 Universal1.0 (CC0-1.0) |

CC-BY-4.0 legal terms: [Creative Commons legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode). Attribution: Blast Radius contributors; include artifact title, version, source location and a change notice. No endorsement or certification is conveyed.

CC0-1.0 terms: [Creative Commons public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode). To the extent permitted by law, contributors dedicate their original generated dataset rights to the public domain under CC0; its fallback licence applies where a waiver is ineffective. No contributor can waive someone else's privacy, contractual rights or copyright. Real dataset publication still requires the independent governance/privacy gates.

Apache-2.0 grants in LICENSE do not override the explicit CC-BY terms of the normative artifacts listed here. Conformance implementation code is Apache-2.0 even though the test definitions it consumes are CC-BY-4.0. Synthetic experiment data are plainly labeled so they cannot be mistaken for real submitted records. No credential secret, private HMAC key or third-party full-text report is part of the licence grant.

For future contributions, obtain contributor/employer authority and explicit terms compatible with the affected artifact. The founder's employer affiliation is disclosed, not treated as proof of employer authorization. OpenSSF's default specification/data licences require a compatibility decision; they are not silently substituted here.
